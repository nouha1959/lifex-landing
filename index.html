What LifeX will be

A place to post videos of your longevity, health, and wellness journey, swap cooking tips, and show how the products and services you use actually affect your life. Brands and vendors will be there too — engaging with their audience, showcasing what they offer, and hearing honest feedback from real people, not just influencers.

Health creators will be able to share what works for them and get genuine feedback from like-minded people on the same road.
